What VeriThesis is, and is not

VeriThesis is designed to surface unsupported claims and make unresolved work visible — it does not guarantee that every claim it produces is correct. Source databases, extraction, and model interpretation can all fail in ways the pipeline doesn't catch.

VeriThesis does not replace: your supervisor's review, institutional ethics approval, your institution's submission requirements, or your own responsibility for what you ultimately submit.

Credits and refunds

Credits are charged per real unit of work. When a claim can't be verified after two automatic revision attempts, the verification charge for that specific claim is refunded automatically. The credits already spent on the two revision attempts are not refunded, since that work genuinely happened. Full refunds for a purchased package go through the standard app-store refund process, not an in-app button.

Supervisors

A supervisor you invite can view your plan, integrity scorecard, and flagged claims, and leave comments. A supervisor cannot approve a plan, edit a draft, or delete a project — those stay with you.

Account

The app works anonymously by default. Purchases require linking a real email and password to your account, since paid work needs to be recoverable if you change devices or reinstall.
