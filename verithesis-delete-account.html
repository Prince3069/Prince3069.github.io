Delete your VeriThesis account and data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time — whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

Send an email to support@princedevlabs.com with the subject line "Delete my VeriThesis account," from the email address linked to your account (or, if your account is still anonymous and was never linked to an email, include your account's user ID — found under Settings in the app, if you still have access to it).

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Your account and sign-in credentialsYes
All your projects — plans, sources, evidence, drafted chapters, integrity reportsYes
Uploaded datasets and scanned documentsYes
Credit and purchase historyYes
Comments a supervisor left on your projectsYes

Turnaround time

Requests are processed within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

What this doesn't affect

Purchase records already reported to Google Play or Apple for tax and accounting purposes are retained by those platforms independently, per their own policies — VeriThesis does not control that data.
