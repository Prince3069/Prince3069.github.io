Propsure Account and Data Deletion

Propsure is a property rental platform operated by Prince Dev Labs Limited. Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to: support@princedevlabs.com with the subject: Account Deletion Request.

Include the email address associated with your Propsure account.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Account profile information
  • Name, email address and phone number
  • Property listings
  • Uploaded property photos
  • Saved preferences
  • Messages and activity associated with the account

Data Retention

Certain records may be retained for legal, fraud prevention, security, or regulatory compliance purposes for up to 90 days before permanent deletion.

Contact

For questions, contact support@princedevlabs.com.
