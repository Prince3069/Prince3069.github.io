Goldwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what personal information the Goldwise Android app (package com.goldwise.app) collects, why, and what your choices are. Goldwise is published by Prince Dev Labs Limited, Abuja, Nigeria ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the information described here. Questions: support@princedevlabs.com.

Goldwise is an educational service. It offers human-written market analysis of gold (XAUUSD), courses, quizzes and learning tools. It is not a broker, it does not hold your money and it does not place trades for you.

In short
  • We collect what we need to run your account, your learning progress and your subscription.
  • We do not sell your information, and Goldwise shows no ads.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • Your trade journal is private to you.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or from our account deletion page.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show signals, courses and tools, and to count your free allowance and check your subscription.
  • To send notifications you have allowed, such as new signals and results. You can switch these off in the app or in your phone settings.
  • To keep Goldwise safe: limiting repeated requests, applying the device limit, preventing the reuse of purchases, and investigating misuse.
  • To understand in aggregate how the app is used and to fix problems.
  • To answer your messages and to meet legal obligations.

3. Our legal basis

Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 applies, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (running your account and subscription); our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and improving the app, balanced against your rights; your consent for push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time; and legal obligations where they apply.

4. Who we share it with

We use service providers who process information on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Google LLC and Google Cloud, for Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (our database), Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging (notifications), Cloud Storage, App Check and Analytics.
  • Google Play, for billing and subscription management.

We do not sell or rent your information. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people or our rights, or to a successor if the business is transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply.

5. Transfers outside Nigeria

Google stores and processes data on servers that may be outside Nigeria. Google commits to data protection terms with its customers, and we choose providers that apply security and contractual safeguards for such transfers.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Database rules make sure you can only read your own personal data. Staff access is limited and recorded. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to give you access to your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or provide it in a portable form, and you may withdraw consent you have given. You can delete your account yourself in the app, or ask us by email. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) or to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Children

Goldwise is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has registered, email us and we will delete the account.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date and version above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
